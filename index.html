                  Method

                  Every status cites a source. Thin evidence looks thin.

                  One matrix

                  Each cell takes the best status across all products, and links to its sources. We read two kinds of source: research and regulation, and what companies actually ship.

                  Sure · Low confidence

                  Doubt is drawn, not hidden

                  When the sources are unclear, a cell takes the lower status.

                  Every category weighs the same

                  A category scores the mean of its cells' points. A country scores the mean of its categories. Live, Limited and Announced cells must cite a source, or the build fails.
