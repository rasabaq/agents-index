          Method

          Inputs are easy to count. Outcomes are what matter. We measure outcomes.

          Two lenses

          Research. Reports, papers and regulation, such as central-bank rules, open-finance timelines and digital-ID laws.

          Companies. What builders actually ship, and where: supported-country pages, launch posts and developer docs. Every company is recorded with its HQ and live markets.
